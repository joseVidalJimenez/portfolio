Welcome to my portfolio!

As a design engineer, I specialise in creating and refining bespoke mechanical solutions, constantly striving to explore innovative approaches and implement out-of-the-box ideas. My journey began with a deep curiosity about how things work, leading me to a career focused on early-stage development, where I translate requirements and specifications into meaningful, functional systems.

Recently, my passion for engineering has taken a new direction as I immerse myself in the field of mechatronics — where mechanical design seamlessly integrates with electronic systems. I am eager to apply my expertise in mechanical design, along with my growing knowledge of electronics, to push the boundaries of what's possible.
