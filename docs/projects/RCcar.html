WombleBot — RC Car

Company: Personal project

Description: Built my first RC car entirely from scavenged scrap parts — code-named "WombleBot" after the Wombles, who famously make use of things left behind. The project served as a hands-on introduction to vehicle electronics, motor control, and chassis design.

My Role: Sourced and repurposed scrap components, designed and assembled the chassis, wired the drive electronics, and programmed the control system. Every part of the build was improvised from available materials.

Outcome: A fully functional RC car built from scrap, demonstrating that working systems can be built resourcefully. A practical exercise in electronics integration, problem-solving, and making things work with constraints.
