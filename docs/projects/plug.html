Universal Cable Adapter (UCA)

Company: Mauro Campisi / Makers Department

Description: Developed a power cord with an integrated power supply adapter compatible with various international sockets, adhering to IEC-60320 standards.

My Role: Conducted research, disassembled existing products, and designed a rotary switch prototype that acted as a connector selector. This involved iterating through designs and creating prototypes using 3D printing and hand-made brass components.

Outcome: Developed a functional prototype that integrated seamlessly with international connectors, improving upon existing designs.
