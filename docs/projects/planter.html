Targeted Irrigation System

Company: Invireo

Description: Designed an indoor targeted irrigation system featuring a motorised arm with 2D movement across a planting area. The system was intended to differentially irrigate sections of the planting area based on the specific water needs of various plants.

My Role: I designed the Cartesian mechanism that moved the arm over the planting area. The main challenge was fitting all components, including motors, within an already designed case. This required multiple iterations to merge parts into fewer components, following TRIZ Principle #6: Universality. Additionally, I minimised the mechanism's footprint to maximise the planting area.

Outcome: Successfully developed a compact and efficient irrigation system that met the project's goals.
